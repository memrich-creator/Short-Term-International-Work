Trip Tracking Calculator

ℹ️ Information: This calculator helps you track your international work trips against the 45-day annual rolling limit. Add your previous trips to see your current usage status and plan future trips.

📋 How to Use This Calculator:

  • Add Previous Trip to Tracker (Green Button): Use for trips you've already completed. These count toward your 45-day limit and appear in Request Tracking.
  • Add Trip to Calculator (Blue Button): Use for planning future trips. These stay in the calculator for testing usage impact without affecting your official record.
  • Status Cards: Show your current usage, remaining days, and usage status in real-time.
  • Rolling 12-Month Window: Only trips within the last 12 months count toward your 45-day limit.

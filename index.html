Trip Tracking Calculator

Add trips to calculate your usage against the 45-day annual limit. Use the buttons below based on your trip type.

ℹ️ Information: This calculator helps you track your Short Term International Work trips against the 45-day annual rolling limit. Add your previous trips to see your current usage status and plan future trips for experimentation and testing.

How to Use This Calculator:

  • Add Previous Trip to Tracker (Green Button): Use for trips you've already taken or that have been approved by your manager. These count toward your 45-day limit and appear in Request Tracking.
  • Add Trip to Calculator (Blue Button): Use for planning future trips and experimentation. These stay in the calculator for testing usage impact without affecting your official record.
  • Status Cards: Show your current usage, remaining days, and usage status in real-time.
  • Rolling 12-Month Window: Only trips within the last 12 months count toward your 45-day limit.
